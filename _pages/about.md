I received my Ph.D. degree from the School of Artificial Intelligence (SAI), Jilin University in 2026. Before that, I began my graduate studies at SAI in 2019 and transitioned directly from the master’s program to the Ph.D. program in 2021. I obtained my bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering (Excellent Engineer Class) from Jilin University in 2019 (RANK: 1/30). During my doctoral studies, I worked as an exchange student at Hong Kong Baptist University under the supervision of Prof. Jing Ma, and as a visiting scholar at Michigan State University under the supervision of Prof. Jiliang Tang, each for one year. I have authored more than 10 publications, including 5 first-author papers in CCF/CAAI A venues.
